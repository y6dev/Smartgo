Frequently asked questions

Is SmartGoCast free?

Yes. SmartGoCast is free to download on Google Play. It is supported by ads.

How do I mirror my Android phone to my TV?

Connect the phone and the TV to the same Wi‑Fi network, open SmartGoCast, wait for your TV to appear, tap it, then choose screen mirroring or the content you want to cast.

Which TVs work with SmartGoCast?

Samsung Smart TVs, LG Smart TVs, Android TV and Google TV, Chromecast devices and DLNA-compatible receivers. Most recent smart TVs and wireless display adapters are supported.

Do I need a cable or an HDMI adapter?

No. Everything goes through your home Wi‑Fi network.

Is there an iPhone version?

SmartGoCast is currently available for Android phones and tablets on Google Play.

Does SmartGoCast include IPTV channels?

No. SmartGoCast is a player: it does not host, provide or sell playlists or channels. You use your own valid M3U playlist or stream link.

Why can't the app find my TV?

Check that both devices are on the same Wi‑Fi network (not a guest network), that the TV is switched on, and restart the scan. On some routers, the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands must be the same network.
