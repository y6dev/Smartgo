Cast your Android phone to a Samsung Smart TV

Samsung Smart TVs can receive videos, photos and screen mirroring from an Android phone over Wi‑Fi. SmartGoCast finds your Samsung TV on the network and sends your content in a few taps, whatever the brand of your phone.

Get it on Google PlayFree on Android · Contains ads

Step by step

  1. Turn on the TVSwitch on your Samsung TV and make sure it is connected to your home Wi‑Fi (Settings › General › Network).
  2. Same networkConnect your Android phone to the same Wi‑Fi network as the TV.
  3. Open SmartGoCastLaunch the app and let it scan. Your Samsung TV appears in the list of devices.
  4. Allow the connectionSelect the TV. If a prompt appears on the TV screen, accept it with the remote.
  5. Start castingChoose screen mirroring, a video, photos, a web page or your IPTV playlist.

Chromecast on a Samsung TV?

For a long time Samsung TVs had no Chromecast built in. Samsung has started adding Google Cast to its 2026 models and to some older models through software updates. If your TV has received it, Cast-compatible apps will show it directly.

To check, update your TV (Settings › Support › Software Update) and look for it behind the Cast button of an app such as YouTube. If it doesn't appear, you don't need to buy a Chromecast: SmartGoCast sends videos, photos and IPTV to Samsung TVs using the protocols they already support.

Tips for a smooth picture on Samsung TVs

  • Use the 5 GHz Wi‑Fi band when your router offers it: it carries video with less lag than 2.4 GHz.
  • Keep the phone reasonably close to the router during long videos, and close apps you are not using.
  • If the TV does not show up, turn it off and on again from the remote, then restart the scan in SmartGoCast.
  • For films and series, casting a video plays it directly on the TV and saves your phone's battery compared with full screen mirroring.

Frequently asked questions

Does SmartGoCast work with all Samsung TVs?

It works with most Samsung Smart TVs connected to Wi‑Fi, including recent Tizen-based models. Very old non-smart models without network access cannot receive a cast.

Do I need a Samsung phone?

No. Any Android phone or tablet that runs SmartGoCast can cast to a Samsung TV.

Is mirroring to a Samsung TV free?

Yes, SmartGoCast is a free download on Google Play, supported by ads.

Can I activate Chromecast on my Samsung TV?

Only if your model has received Google Cast through an update, which Samsung is rolling out to recent TVs. Otherwise, SmartGoCast lets you cast from your Android phone without a Chromecast.
