Cast to Android TV, Google TV and Chromecast

Android TV boxes, Google TV televisions and Chromecast dongles are made to receive casts from a phone. SmartGoCast detects them on your Wi‑Fi along with DLNA receivers, so one app covers every screen in the house.

Step by step

  1. Plug in the receiverMake sure your Android TV, Google TV or Chromecast is set up and online.
  2. Same Wi‑FiConnect your phone to the same Wi‑Fi network as the receiver.
  3. Find the deviceOpen SmartGoCast. The receiver appears with its name in the device list.
  4. ConnectTap the device to connect.
  5. CastChoose a video, photos, a web page, your IPTV playlist or screen mirroring.

Chromecast, Android TV or DLNA: what's the difference?

  • Chromecast is a small dongle that plugs into the HDMI port of any TV and turns it into a casting target.
  • Android TV and Google TV are complete TV systems found in many Sony, TCL, Philips and Xiaomi televisions and boxes, with casting built in.
  • DLNA is an older, widely supported standard for playing media over a home network. Many TVs, set-top boxes and media players support it.
  • SmartGoCast lists all of them in one place, so you don't need to know which protocol your TV uses.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Chromecast to use SmartGoCast?

No. A Chromecast is one option. Samsung, LG, Android TV, Google TV and DLNA TVs work without any extra device.

Does it work with Google TV?

Yes, Google TV devices are supported.

Can I use it with a TV box?

Yes, Android TV boxes and DLNA-compatible media players connected to your Wi‑Fi can receive casts.
