IPTV player: cast your M3U playlist to the TV

SmartGoCast includes an IPTV player that reads your own M3U playlists and stream links, then sends the channel to your Smart TV. It is handy for TVs that have no IPTV app or where installing one is complicated.

Get it on Google PlayFree on Android · Contains ads

Step by step

  1. Have your link readyCopy the M3U playlist URL or stream link provided by your legal service.
  2. Add it to SmartGoCastOpen the IPTV section of the app and paste or import your playlist.
  3. Connect your TVWith phone and TV on the same Wi‑Fi, pick your TV in the device list.
  4. Choose a channelBrowse the playlist and tap a channel.
  5. Watch on the TVThe stream plays on the big screen while your phone works as the remote.

Good to know before you start

  • SmartGoCast is a player only. It does not host, provide or sell any playlist, channel or copyrighted content.
  • Use playlists from services you are entitled to use, such as your provider's official stream or free legal channels.
  • A stable Wi‑Fi connection matters more for live streams than for files: the 5 GHz band gives the best results.
  • Some stream formats are not supported by every TV. If one channel won't play, try another or check your provider's link.

Frequently asked questions

Does SmartGoCast provide IPTV channels?

No. You must use your own valid M3U playlist or stream link.

Which playlist formats are supported?

M3U playlists and direct stream links.

Can I watch IPTV on a Samsung or LG TV without installing an app on the TV?

Yes. SmartGoCast plays the stream from your phone and casts it to the TV, so nothing needs to be installed on the television.
