TV or Chromecast not showing up? How to fix it

When your TV doesn't appear in the list of devices, the cause is almost always the network, not the TV. Go through these checks in order: most problems are solved in the first three.

Step by step

  1. Use the same Wi‑Fi networkPhone and TV must be on exactly the same network. A guest network, a Wi‑Fi repeater with its own name or a mobile hotspot keeps devices apart.
  2. Turn off VPN and mobile dataDisable your VPN and, for the test, mobile data, so the phone doesn't send traffic outside your home network.
  3. Restart the TV completelyUnplug the TV or Chromecast for 30 seconds. Standby doesn't always reset the network connection.
  4. Check app permissionsOn Android, allow SmartGoCast to find nearby devices (Settings › Apps › SmartGoCast › Permissions). Without it, the scan finds nothing.
  5. Check the routerTurn off 'AP isolation' or 'client isolation'. If your router shows 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz as two networks, connect both devices to the same one.
  6. Update the TV and the appInstall pending updates on the TV and on your phone, then scan again.

Checks by device

  • Chromecast and Google TV: in the Google Home app, check that the device is online and on the same Wi‑Fi. If it doesn't appear there either, set up its Wi‑Fi again.
  • Samsung TV: switch the TV on with the remote, as some models don't answer from deep standby, and check the connection in Settings › General › Network.
  • LG TV: check that network sharing options are enabled in the connection settings.
  • Other TVs and boxes: make sure DLNA or media sharing is enabled. The name of the option varies by brand.

Still not found?

Restart your router, wait two minutes, then restart the TV and then the phone. If the TV still doesn't appear, write to the support address at the bottom of this page with your TV model and phone model.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my Chromecast not showing up?

Usually because the phone and the Chromecast are on different networks, or the router isolates devices. Put both on the same Wi‑Fi, turn off VPN and guest network, then restart the Chromecast.

Does casting need an internet connection?

The devices must be on the same local network. Content from the internet, such as web videos or IPTV, also needs a working connection. Your own photos and videos only need the local network.

My TV appears but the video doesn't play. Why?

Some TVs don't support every video format. Try another video, or use screen mirroring, which works with any content.
